Privacy

Last updated 25 July 2026

Carve has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is stored on your own device and never sent to us — we do not operate a server that could receive it.

What is stored on your device

The game saves to your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and is readable only by this site:

  • Which sculptures you have carved, and your best star score for each
  • Which level you were last playing
  • Which collections you have unlocked, and whether Zen mode is unlocked
  • Your sound, tone and clue-display preferences

Clearing your browser data for this site erases all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, so that erasure is permanent and we cannot restore it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising identifier, no fingerprinting and no error-reporting service. We do not know how many people play, or who they are.

Other services the page contacts

The 3D engine (three.js) loads from jsDelivr, a public code network. Your browser's request tells jsDelivr your IP address and browser version, as any web request does. jsDelivr states that it does not log personal data or set cookies — see their privacy policy. We receive nothing from this.

Advertising

There are currently no ads in Carve, and no advertising code runs. If that changes, the ad script will not load until you have been asked and have agreed — the game is built so that consent is required before any advertising request is made, not after. This section will be updated before any such change goes live.

Purchases

Collection unlocks are currently simulated locally for testing and take no payment. No payment details are requested, handled or stored. If real purchasing is added, it will run through a payment provider who will handle card details directly, and this page will say who.

Children

Carve is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, we collect none from children either.

Playing on other platforms

A separate build of Carve runs on CrazyGames. That version uses their SDK for saved progress and ads, so their privacy policy governs it, not this one. This page covers the version you are reading it on.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, erasure and portability apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to return or delete. The data on your device is already entirely under your control, and the game has an export option so you can keep your own copy.
