How to play

Pull blocks out of the tower without hitting the mines hidden inside it.

  1. 1

    Pull a block

    Tap a block to pull it out. Drag anywhere to spin the tower — blocks hide on all four sides.

  2. 2

    Read the gap

    The number left behind counts the mines touching that block — including the layers above and below it. That’s up to 8.

    Tap a number to light up the blocks it’s counting.

  3. 3

    Mark your suspects

    Press and hold a block to flag it. Flagged blocks are locked, so you can’t lose a heart to a mis-tap. Hold again to unflag.

  4. 4

    Mind your hearts

    Hit a mine and it turns red and stays put, costing you a heart. Lose all three and the run ends.
