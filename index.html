Ads in Carve

Carve can show an optional ad when a carve cracks, to let you keep going. Ads come from Google and set cookies to pick which ad to show.

Say no and the game plays exactly the same — you still get the same chances to keep carving. Nothing else about Carve tracks you. You can change this any time under Display.
